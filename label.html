Filler labelling

Builds the dataset for the in-browser filler detector (F15 in docs/FEATURES.md). Record or open takes, mark every um, uh and other hard filler, then export. Dev tool — it is never deployed, and nothing leaves this page except the files you download.

Record a take

Same mic settings as the app (echo cancellation and noise suppression on, auto-gain off), so the training audio sounds like what the detector will hear. Talk naturally — don't perform fillers.

Or open files

Audio files, plus their .json labels to carry on where you left off.
